Groups and Bands

I regularly perform with a variety of musicians and groups. My philosophy is to listen and choose the best fit for the sound you're looking for. From cocktail bars and jazz brunches with my duo; big stages with my six-piece band; singing and playing guitar at bars and restaurants; to however I can best complement your sound with my keys, I am a versatile player that can do whatever you need me to.

Teresa and Ben

Part of the regularly gigging Duo Teresa and Ben, performing together in a variety of settings, specializing in jazz versions of your favorite popular songs.

Butter

Keys player in Butter, a six-piece jazz/funk/soul group with that smooth exciting sound!

Other Collaborations

I am also a regular contributor to many other groups and musical projects, performing and collaborating solo and with musicians throughout the Greater Boston community and beyond.

Composition

My work as a composer ranges from orchestral original compositions and arrangements; to theme music for movies, TV, and commercials; to interactive music for videogame projects. Whatever your project may be, let me help you illustrate it with sound.

Master's Degree in Composition

Master's Degree in Composition from the Longy School of Music of Bard College, with a focus on orchestral arrangements, original music, music theory, and videogame music.
